# Sport Specific Rules
We have three main buckets of sports: 
Grappling Sports (Wrestling, Judo, BJJ)
Striking (Muay Thai, Boxing, Kickboxing)
MMA (Mixed, All included) 

Fundamentally, the preparation is similar, but there will be differences in terms of priority.

Grappling is very strength and power based, and to a secondary degree, raw speed. 
Striking is very speed based, and to a secondary degree, raw strength and power. 
MMA is somewhere in between. 

The programming needs to reflect this. Meaning that periodization is done appropriately so they display the most needed qualities by the time of the competition. 

For example, a progression (periodization model depending on season or camp) for strikers could be: 
High volume strength > High intensity Strength > Raw Speed/Explosive
