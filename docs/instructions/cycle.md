Each Cycle
After the initial questionnaire (above) is done, each cycle will as a general rule last 12 weeks. 

UNLESS: they have specifically written an important event (e.g., a competition) that finishes before 12 weeks. Then the app will adjust for peaking towards that, and an assessment comes after that completion… for a new period. 

But in general… 12 weeks as the default cycle.
One 12-week cycle
 broken into:
Weeks 1–4
Weeks 5–8
Weeks 9–12
Then at the end of Week 12:
athlete gives new input
app reviews progress
next 12-week cycle is generated
So again…
At the start of the 12-week cycle
Athlete inputs:
sport
emphasis: endurance / strength-power / both
current training load
equipment
current limitations
primary goal
off-camp or in-camp
competition date if any
During the cycle
Do not wait 12 full weeks to adjust anything.
Have:
small auto-adjustments weekly
formal review at Week 4
formal review at Week 8
full re-input at Week 12
That is the key.
So the logic becomes:
Weekly: autoregulate loads, reps, conditioning dose
Every 4 weeks: check whether the block is working
Every 12 weeks: regenerate the bigger plan
Suggested system
12-week parent cycle
 with
4-week check-ins
12-week rebuild
What happens at each checkpoint
Week 4 check-in
Ask:
Is recovery manageable?
Is the current goal still the same?
Has combat-sport load changed?
Any pain or movement issue?
Are lifts/conditioning trending up, flat, or down?
Then, following options are done depending on what they answer: 
keep course
slightly reduce volume
slightly increase emphasis
Week 8 check-in
Ask the same.
Then:
continue
deload and resume
transition to peaking block
reduce general work, increase specificity
Week 12 re-input
This is the real reset.
Ask for updated:
goals
schedule
training availability
current sport load
equipment
pain/injury status
upcoming event info
Again, for in-camp athletes, the app should not force a full 12-week structure if the fight is sooner.
So:
if no competition is planned: default to 12 weeks
if competition is in 6, 8, or 10 weeks, or built to whatever the competition date is instead.
Final answer
Use 12 weeks as the default cycle length.
 But build in:
weekly autoregulation
check-ins every 4 weeks
full updated athlete input at Week 12
